Kyle's TPU doesn't flow cleanly when the nozzle hops between parts on one plate: it leaves hundreds of fine strands between them (Kyle, 2026-10-08, printing the conveyor cones).

How to apply: when a bench doc has him print more than one TPU part, tell him to use Bambu Studio's Print sequence → By object. On his A1 the bar the print head rides on sits 25 mm above the nozzle, so taller parts can't sit side by side: line them up front to back, about 10 cm apart centre to centre. That fits three of the 61–72 mm cones per plate. Bambu Studio warns when parts are too close or too tall.

On 2026-10-09 a TPU cone tore coming off the textured PEI plate, even with the spatula. For any TPU print, tell him to glue the plate as a release layer. Flexing the plate does nothing on parts this small, even with tree supports: freeze the plate for about 10 minutes, then peel the brim up from its outer edge. If he used glue, warm water dissolves it and frees the part. Related: [[user_printing_tree_supports]], [[project_tendwright]].
