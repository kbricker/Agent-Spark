Kyle's tool inventory, as he gave it on 2026-11-22. The Bosch part numbers were checked against Bosch and retailer listings.

- **Digital meter.** In Kyle's words, "you have the digital meter."
  - It reads distance and tilt by laser.
  - It is 53 mm wide, so the beam runs 26.5 mm off its narrow edge.
  - Corner angles come from the digital angle meter, whose two arms lie along the corner's edges.
- **Jigsaw.** "its a Bosch JS260".
- **Router.** Kyle called it "the router RA 1161". It is a Bosch 1617/1618-series motor; he hasn't given the motor's model.
  - **RA1161** is the fixed base. With its subbase off it mounts under a router table, and the T-wrench sets the bit depth from above.
  - **RA1166** is the plunge base, Kyle's "manual frame". It plunges up to 2 5/16".
  - **RA1181** is the router table: a 27" × 18" aluminum benchtop table with a tall aluminum fence faced in MDF.
  - **RA1054** is the deluxe edge guide. It also works as a circle guide up to 32" across.
  - **RBS010** is Kyle's "blade kit": ten carbide bits with ½" shanks, made up of:
    - three straight bits
    - two surface-forming bits
    - one joinery bit
    - three edge-forming bits, one of them a 45° chamfer (85597M, 1-5/16" across, bearing-guided)
    - one trimming bit
